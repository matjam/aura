# Shorthand expansion: inline styles parsed and serialized as longhands,
# and shorthand values read back from the block. Longhands come out in the
# order the expander emits them (border goes side by side; background
# puts the color last). Background positions are stored resolved to
# percentages and calc() offsets from the left or top edge.
