Inline formatting: line breaking, white-space, alignment, vertical-align,
inline-block, spacing. Ahem: every glyph is 1em square; 16px font gives
16px lines with the baseline 12.8px down.

=== words wrap at the container width
<body style="margin:0;width:100px">
<div id=t>xx xx xx xx</div>
---
#t h=32 lines=2

=== a long word overflows instead of breaking
<body style="margin:0;width:50px">
<div id=t>xxxxxxxx xx</div>
---
#t h=32 lines=2

=== overflow-wrap anywhere breaks a long word
<body style="margin:0;width:50px">
<div id=t style="overflow-wrap:anywhere">xxxxxxxx</div>
---
#t h=48 lines=3

=== word-break break-all
<body style="margin:0;width:64px">
<div id=t style="word-break:break-all">xx xxxxxx</div>
---
#t lines=3

=== white-space nowrap keeps one line
<body style="margin:0;width:50px">
<div id=t style="white-space:nowrap">xx xx xx xx</div>
---
#t h=16 lines=1

=== pre keeps newlines and spaces
<body style="margin:0">
<pre id=t style="margin:0">x
  x

x</pre>
---
// pre is monospace: 13px by default
#t h=52 lines=4

=== pre-wrap wraps and keeps spaces
<body style="margin:0;width:80px">
<div id=t style="white-space:pre-wrap">xx   xx xx</div>
---
#t lines=2

=== pre-line collapses spaces and keeps newlines
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=t style="white-space:pre-line">x    x
x</div>
---
#t lines=2

=== br forces a break
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=t>x<br>x<br><br>x</div>
---
#t h=64 lines=4

=== collapsible whitespace between blocks makes no line
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=t>
   <div style="height:10px"></div>
   </div>
---
#t h=10

=== text-align centre and right
<body style="margin:0;width:100px">
<div id=c style="text-align:center"><span id=a style="display:inline-block;width:20px;height:5px"></span></div>
<div id=r style="text-align:right"><span id=b style="display:inline-block;width:20px;height:5px"></span></div>
---
#a x=40
#b x=80

=== text-align justify stretches all but the last line
<body style="margin:0;width:120px">
<div style="text-align:justify">xx <span id=s style="display:inline-block;width:16px;height:16px"></span> xx xx</div>
<div style="text-align:justify">xx <span id=t style="display:inline-block;width:16px;height:16px"></span> xx</div>
---
#s x=52
#t x=48

=== text-indent
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="text-indent:30px"><span id=a style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></div>
---
#a x=30

=== letter-spacing and word-spacing
<body style="margin:0">
<div>xx<span id=a style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></div>
<div style="letter-spacing:2px">xx<span id=b style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></div>
<div style="word-spacing:10px">x x<span id=c style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></div>
---
#a x=32
#b x=36
#c x=58

=== inline-block sits on the baseline
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p>x<span id=ib style="display:inline-block;width:20px;height:40px"></span></div>
---
#ib y=0 h=40
#p h=43.2

=== inline-block baseline is its last line
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p>x<span id=ib style="display:inline-block;width:40px">x<br>x</span></div>
---
#ib y=0 h=32
#p h=32

=== inline-block with overflow hidden uses its bottom margin edge
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p>x<span id=ib style="display:inline-block;overflow:hidden;width:40px">x<br>x</span></div>
---
#ib y=0
#p h=35.2

=== vertical-align top and bottom
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p>x<span id=a style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:40px"></span><span id=t style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:10px;vertical-align:top"></span><span id=b style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:10px;vertical-align:bottom"></span></div>
---
#t y=0
#b y=33.2

=== a tall bottom-aligned box pushes the baseline group down
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p>x<span style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:40px;vertical-align:bottom"></span><span id=a style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:5px"></span></div>
<div id=q>x<span style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:40px;vertical-align:top"></span><span id=c style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:5px"></span></div>
---
// bottom: the line's top moves up, so the baseline is at 40 - 3.2
#p h=40
#a y=31.8
// top: the line's bottom moves down; the baseline stays at 12.8
#c y=47.8

=== vertical-align middle
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="line-height:100px">x<span id=m style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:20px;vertical-align:middle"></span></div>
---
// Ahem's x-height is 0.8em: the middle is 6.4px above the baseline (54.8).
#m y=38.4

=== vertical-align length and percentage
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="line-height:20px">x<span id=a style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:10px;vertical-align:5px"></span></div>
---
// Raised 5px its top is 0.2px above the strut: the line grows.
#a y=0

=== vertical-align sub and super move the box
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p style="line-height:16px">x<span id=s style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:10px;vertical-align:super"></span></div>
---
// super raises by font-size / 3 + 1 (as WebKit).
#p h=19.533

=== line-height sets the line box height
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="line-height:30px">x</div>
<div id=b style="line-height:2">x</div>
<div id=c style="line-height:normal">x</div>
---
#a h=30
#b h=32
#c h=16

=== a taller inline font grows the line through the strut
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p style="line-height:1">x<span style="font-size:32px">x</span></div>
<div id=q style="line-height:1"><span style="font-size:8px;vertical-align:top">x</span></div>
<div id=r style="line-height:20px"><span style="font-size:32px;line-height:1;vertical-align:-10px">x</span></div>
---
#p h=32
#q h=16
// strut: 14.8 above the baseline, 5.2 below; span: 15.6 above, 16.4 below
#r h=32

=== inline padding and borders do not change the line height
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p>x<span style="padding:10px;border:5px solid">x</span><span id=a style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></div>
---
#p h=16
#a x=62

=== split inline boxes across lines
<body style="margin:0;width:100px">
<div id=p>x <span id=s style="padding:0 5px">xx xx xx</span></div>
---
#p lines=2

=== soft hyphen breaks
<body style="margin:0;width:80px">
<div id=t>xxx&shy;xxx</div>
---
#t lines=2

=== wbr gives a break opportunity
<body style="margin:0;width:80px">
<div id=t>xxx<wbr>xxx</div>
---
#t lines=2

=== tab size in pre
<body style="margin:0">
<pre style="margin:0;tab-size:4">	<span id=a style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></pre>
---
// 4 spaces of the 13px monospace default
#a x=52

=== text-transform does not change Ahem widths
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="text-transform:uppercase">xx<span id=a style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></div>
---
#a x=32

=== line-clamp limits lines
<body style="margin:0;width:100px">
<div id=t style="display:-webkit-box;-webkit-box-orient:vertical;-webkit-line-clamp:2;overflow:hidden">xx xx xx xx xx xx xx xx</div>
---
#t h=32

=== an image in a line sits on the baseline
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p>x<img id=i width=20 height=30></div>
---
#i y=0
#p h=33.2

=== an empty inline-block
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p><span style="display:inline-block;width:10px;height:10px"></span></div>
---
#p h=16

=== text-overflow ellipsis keeps one line
<body style="margin:0;width:50px">
<div id=t style="white-space:nowrap;overflow:hidden;text-overflow:ellipsis">xxxxxxxxxx</div>
---
#t h=16 w=50 lines=1

=== hit test on text returns its element
<body style="margin:0">
<p style="margin:0">aa <b id=b>bb</b> cc</p>
---
hit 60 8 #b
hit 10 8 <p>

=== a tab advances to the next tab stop
<body style="margin:0">
<pre style="margin:0">x	x	<span id=s>x</span></pre>
---
// 13px monospace: tab stops every 8 x 13 = 104px
#s x=208
pixel 6 6 000000
pixel 40 6 ffffff
pixel 110 6 000000
pixel 150 6 ffffff
pixel 214 6 000000

<div style="color:transparent;text-decoration:line-through wavy #0000ff 2px">xxxx</div>
