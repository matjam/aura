Intrinsic sizing: min-content, max-content, fit-content, shrink-to-fit.

=== inline-block shrinks to its content
<body style="margin:0">
<span id=a style="display:inline-block">xx xxx</span>
---
#a w=96 h=16

=== inline-block narrower than its max-content wraps
<body style="margin:0;width:60px">
<div><span id=a style="display:inline-block">xx xxx</span></div>
---
#a w=60 h=32

=== width min-content and max-content
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="width:min-content">xx xxx x</div>
<div id=b style="width:max-content">xx xxx x</div>
<div id=c style="width:fit-content">xx xxx x</div>
---
#a w=48 h=48
#b w=128 h=16
#c w=128

=== fit-content clamps at the available width
<body style="margin:0;width:100px">
<div id=c style="width:fit-content">xx xxx x</div>
---
#c w=100

=== intrinsic width includes padding, border, margins of children
<body style="margin:0">
<span id=a style="display:inline-block"><div style="width:50px;padding:0 5px;border:0 solid;border-left-width:2px;margin-right:10px;height:1px"></div></span>
---
#a w=72

=== intrinsic width of floats side by side
<body style="margin:0">
<span id=a style="display:inline-block"><div style="float:left;width:30px;height:5px"></div><div style="float:left;width:40px;height:5px"></div></span>
---
#a w=70

=== percentage children do not contribute
<body style="margin:0">
<span id=a style="display:inline-block"><div style="width:50%;height:5px"></div>xx</span>
---
#a w=32

=== word-break break-word lets min-content break inside a word
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="width:min-content;word-break:break-word">xx xxxxxxxx</div>
<div id=b style="width:min-content;overflow-wrap:anywhere">xxxxxxxx</div>
<div id=c style="width:min-content;overflow-wrap:break-word">xxxxxxxx</div>
---
// masnick.com: a long Bluesky URL under word-break: break-word set the
// min-content of a 1fr grid column, pushing the sidebar off the page
#a w=16
#b w=16
#c w=128

=== nowrap text max is min
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="width:min-content;white-space:nowrap">xx xx</div>
---
#a w=80

=== intrinsic size of a table placeholder
<body style="margin:0">
<table id=t style="border-spacing:0"><tr><td style="padding:0">xx</td><td style="padding:0">xxx</td></tr></table>
---
#t w=80 h=16

=== min-width auto of an image in an inline-block
<body style="margin:0">
<span id=a style="display:inline-block"><img width=40 height=10>x</span>
---
#a w=56
