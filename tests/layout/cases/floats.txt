Floats and clear (CSS 2.1 9.5).

=== left and right floats
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=l style="float:left;width:100px;height:50px"></div>
<div id=r style="float:right;width:100px;height:50px"></div>
---
#l x=0 y=0
#r x=700 y=0

=== floats stack horizontally
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="float:left;width:100px;height:50px"></div>
<div id=b style="float:left;width:100px;height:50px"></div>
<div id=c style="float:right;width:100px;height:50px"></div>
<div id=d style="float:right;width:100px;height:50px"></div>
---
#a x=0
#b x=100
#c x=700
#d x=600

=== a float that does not fit moves down
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="width:300px">
<div id=a style="float:left;width:200px;height:50px"></div>
<div id=b style="float:left;width:200px;height:30px"></div>
</div>
---
#b x=0 y=50

=== a float moves below the shortest blocking float
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="width:300px">
<div id=a style="float:left;width:100px;height:50px"></div>
<div id=b style="float:left;width:100px;height:20px"></div>
<div id=c style="float:left;width:150px;height:10px"></div>
</div>
---
#c x=100 y=20

=== float margins
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="float:left;width:100px;height:50px;margin:10px 20px"></div>
<div id=b style="float:left;width:100px;height:50px;margin:5px"></div>
---
#a x=20 y=10
#b x=145 y=5

=== text wraps around a float
<body style="margin:0;width:200px">
<div id=f style="float:left;width:100px;height:32px"></div>
<p id=p style="margin:0">xx xx xx xx</p>
---
#p y=0 h=32 lines=2

=== line boxes shorten next to a float
<body style="margin:0;width:200px">
<div style="float:left;width:100px;height:16px"></div>
<div id=t>xxxxxx xxxxxxx</div>
---
#t h=32 lines=2

=== a block's box ignores the float, its lines do not
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="float:left;width:100px;height:100px"></div>
<div id=b style="height:10px"></div>
---
#b x=0 w=800

=== a BFC root avoids floats
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="float:left;width:100px;height:100px"></div>
<div id=b style="overflow:hidden;height:10px"></div>
---
#b x=100 w=700

=== a BFC root too wide for the space goes below
<body style="margin:0;width:300px">
<div style="float:left;width:100px;height:100px"></div>
<div id=b style="overflow:hidden;width:250px;height:10px"></div>
---
#b x=0 y=100

=== a BFC root contains its floats
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=p style="overflow:hidden"><div style="float:left;width:10px;height:70px"></div></div>
<div id=q><div style="float:left;width:10px;height:70px"></div></div>
---
#p h=70
#q h=0

=== clear left, right and both
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="float:left;width:100px;height:40px"></div>
<div style="float:right;width:100px;height:60px"></div>
<div id=a style="clear:left;height:5px"></div>
<div id=b style="clear:right;height:5px"></div>
<div id=c style="clear:both;height:5px"></div>
---
#a y=40
#b y=60
#c y=65

=== a float clears earlier floats
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="float:left;width:100px;height:40px"></div>
<div id=f style="float:left;clear:left;width:100px;height:40px"></div>
---
#f x=0 y=40

=== a float's top is not above an earlier block
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="height:30px"></div>
<div id=f style="float:left;width:10px;height:10px"></div>
---
#f y=30

=== a float is not above an earlier float
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="float:right;width:100px;height:100px"></div>
<div style="float:left;width:750px;height:10px"></div>
<div id=f style="float:left;width:10px;height:10px"></div>
---
#f x=750 y=100

=== a float inside a line
<body style="margin:0;width:300px">
<div id=p>xx<span id=f style="float:right;width:50px;height:50px"></span>xx</div>
---
#f x=250 y=0
#p lines=1

=== a float after text on the line goes beside it when it fits
<body style="margin:0;width:200px">
<div>xxxx <span id=f style="float:left;width:50px;height:10px"></span>yyyy</div>
---
#f x=0 y=0

=== shrink-to-fit float width
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=f style="float:left">xx xxx</div>
<div id=g style="float:left;clear:left;width:min-content">xx xxx</div>
---
#f w=96
#g w=48

=== float with percentage width
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=f style="float:right;width:25%;height:10px"></div>
---
#f x=600 w=200

=== nested float in an inline-block
<body style="margin:0">
<div><span id=ib style="display:inline-block"><span style="float:left;width:30px;height:30px"></span>xx</span></div>
---
#ib w=62 h=30

=== empty block after a float collapses its margins through
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="float:left;width:100px;height:100px"></div>
<div id=e style="margin:20px 0"></div>
<div id=b style="height:10px"></div>
---
#b y=20

=== a line moved below floats does not place its floats twice
<body style="margin:0;width:200px">
<div id=p><div style="float:left;width:150px;height:20px"></div><span id=f style="float:left;width:100px;height:10px"></span>xxxxxxxxxx</div>
<div id=n style="float:left;width:10px;height:10px"></div>
---
#f x=0 y=20
#p h=46 lines=1
