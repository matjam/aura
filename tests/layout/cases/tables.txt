Tables, as far as the placeholder table layout goes (src/layout/table.c):
columns from cell widths, colspan, border-spacing, vertical-align in
cells. Ahem: 16px lines.

=== cells sit side by side in content-sized columns
<body style="margin:0">
<table style="border-spacing:0"><tr><td id=a style="padding:0">xx</td><td id=b style="padding:0">xxx</td></tr></table>
---
#a x=0 w=32
#b x=32 w=48

=== colspan spans columns
<body style="margin:0">
<table style="border-spacing:0"><tr><td style="padding:0">xx</td><td style="padding:0">xx</td></tr>
<tr><td id=c colspan=2 style="padding:0">x</td></tr></table>
---
#c w=64

=== a cell's height property still aligns its content
<body style="margin:0">
<table style="border-spacing:0"><tr>
<td style="height:50px;padding:0;vertical-align:bottom"><span id=b style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></td>
<td style="padding:0;vertical-align:middle"><span id=m style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></td>
<td style="padding:0;vertical-align:top"><span id=t style="display:inline-block;width:5px;height:5px"></span></td>
</tr></table>
---
// content is one 16px line; the row is 50px tall
#b y=41.8
#m y=24.8
#t y=7.8
