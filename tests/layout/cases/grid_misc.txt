Grid: how a grid container takes part in other layout (inline-grid,
flex, shrink-to-fit), absolutely positioned children, stacking, limits.

=== an absolutely positioned child uses its grid area as containing block
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=g style="display:grid;position:relative;width:300px;padding:10px;grid-template-columns:repeat(3,100px);grid-template-rows:50px 50px">
<div id=p style="position:absolute;grid-column:2 / 3;grid-row:2;top:0;left:0;width:20px;height:20px"></div>
<div id=q style="position:absolute;grid-column:2 / 4;grid-row:1 / 2;inset:0"></div>
<div id=a>x</div><div id=b>x</div>
</div>
---
#p x=110 y=60 w=20 h=20
#q x=110 y=10 w=200 h=50
// it takes no cell
#a x=10 y=10
#b x=110 y=10

=== an auto line of an absolutely positioned child is the padding edge
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=g style="display:grid;position:relative;width:300px;padding:10px;grid-template-columns:repeat(3,100px);grid-template-rows:50px 50px">
<div id=p style="position:absolute;grid-column-start:3;inset:0"></div>
<div id=q style="position:absolute;grid-row-end:2;inset:0"></div>
<div id=r style="position:absolute;inset:0"></div>
</div>
---
#p x=210 y=0 w=110 h=120
#q x=0 y=0 w=320 h=60
#r x=0 y=0 w=320 h=120

=== bottom and right offsets measure from the area
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=g style="display:grid;position:relative;width:300px;grid-template-columns:repeat(3,100px);grid-template-rows:50px 50px;gap:10px">
<div id=p style="position:absolute;grid-area:1 / 1 / 2 / 3;right:5px;bottom:5px;width:20px;height:20px"></div>
</div>
---
// the area ends at the end of column 2 (210) and of row 1 (50)
#p x=185 y=25

=== a grid container that is not positioned is not the containing block
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="position:relative;padding:20px;width:400px">
<div id=g style="display:grid;width:300px;grid-template-columns:repeat(3,100px);grid-template-rows:50px">
<div id=p style="position:absolute;grid-column:2;left:0;top:0;width:20px;height:20px"></div>
</div></div>
---
#p x=0 y=0

=== inline-grid sits on a line
<body style="margin:0">
<div>xx<span id=s style="display:inline-grid;grid-template-columns:20px 30px"><i id=a>x</i><i>x</i></span><span id=t>yy</span></div>
---
#s x=32 w=50 h=16
#t x=82

=== inline-grid shrinks to its content
<body style="margin:0">
<div><span id=s style="display:inline-grid;grid-template-columns:auto auto;column-gap:10px"><b>xx</b><b>xxxx</b></span></div>
---
#s w=106

=== a floated grid shrinks to fit its tracks
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=g style="display:grid;float:left;grid-template-columns:50px auto">
<div id=a>x</div><div id=b>xxx</div>
</div>
---
#g w=98
#b x=50 w=48

=== max-content width with fr tracks: every fr holds the largest item
<body style="margin:0">

</div>
---
#g w=128

=== min-content width: fr tracks are as narrow as their items

</div></div>

=== intrinsic width counts spanning items and gutters
<body style="margin:0">

</div>

=== percentage tracks are auto for the intrinsic width, then resolve
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=g style="display:grid;float:left;grid-template-columns:50% 50%">

</div>

=== z-index on an item makes a stacking context without position
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="display:grid;width:100px;grid-template-columns:100px;grid-template-rows:50px">

<div id=b style="grid-area:1 / 1;background:#00f"></div>
</div>
---

pixel 50 25 ff0000

=== overflow on the container: the tracks make the scrollable size
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=g style="display:grid;width:100px;height:50px;overflow:auto;grid-template-columns:150px;grid-template-rows:80px">
<div id=a></div>
</div>
---
#a w=150 h=80
scrollat 10 10 20 20 yes

=== percentage row gap with an indefinite height
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=g style="display:grid;width:100px;grid-template-rows:40px 40px;row-gap:10%">
<div id=a></div><div id=b></div>
</div>
---
// the gap is 0 while the height is found (80), then 10% of it; the rows
// then overflow. Not checked in a browser.
#g h=80
#b y=48
