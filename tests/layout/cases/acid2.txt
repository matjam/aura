Pieces of Acid2 that Aura drew wrong: shrink-to-fit width with a float
between blocks, and the baseline of an empty cell with a height.

=== a float does not add its width to a later block that does not avoid floats
<body style="margin:0">
<div id="a" style="position:absolute"><div style="height:0;text-align:right"><span style="display:inline-block;width:50px;height:10px"></span></div><div style="float:left;width:100px;height:10px"></div><div style="width:100px;height:10px"></div></div>
---
#a w=100

=== an empty cell with a height has its baseline at its content bottom
<body style="margin:0">
<ul id="u" style="display:table;padding:0;margin:0">
<li style="display:table-cell;height:10px;width:10px"></li>
<li style="display:table;height:10px;width:10px"></li>
<li style="display:table-cell;height:5px;width:10px"></li>
<li style="list-style:none;height:10px;width:10px"></li>
</ul>
---
#u h=10

=== a child's top margin collapses into the margin of a block placed by clearance
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="height:10px;margin-bottom:48px"></div>
<div id="f" style="float:left;margin:-24px 24px -12px;width:100px;height:60px"></div>
<div id="e" style="margin:75px"><div style="margin:0 24px -72px 48px"></div></div>
<div id="s" style="margin:60px 36px;clear:both"><div id="c" style="margin-top:3px;height:24px"></div></div>
<div id="n" style="height:5px"></div>
---
// the float's margin box starts after the first block's bottom margin
// (58): its border box is 24 above. Its margin box ends at 58 - 24 + 60
// - 12 = 82. Without clear the second block's border edge would be at
// 10 + (75 - 72) = 13, so clearance places it at 82 (negative clearance:
// 13 + c + 60 = 82), and the child's 3px margin is inside the collapsed
// 60px one.
#f y=34 h=60
#e y=13 h=0
#s y=82 h=24
#c y=82
#n y=166
