Form controls: intrinsic sizes, baselines, centring, fieldset and legend,
details, author restyling. Ahem: every glyph is 1em square (ascent 0.8em).
Controls use 13.333px: a 13.333px line, ascent 10.667. Text inputs have a
2px border and 1px 2px padding; buttons 2px border and 1px 6px padding.

=== text input, button and checkbox share the text baseline
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=d><span id=s>x</span><input id=t value=x><button id=b>x</button><input id=c type=checkbox></div>
---
#d h=22
#s y=3 h=16
#t y=2 w=281 h=20
#b y=2 w=29.33 h=20
#c y=3 w=13 h=13
