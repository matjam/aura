Widths, heights, percentages, auto margins, min/max, box-sizing,
aspect-ratio.

=== auto width fills the containing block
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="height:10px"></div>
<div id=b style="height:10px;margin:0 20px;padding:0 5px;border:3px solid"></div>
---
#a x=0 w=800
#b x=20 w=760 h=16

=== percentage width and padding
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="width:50%;padding:5%;height:10px"></div>
---
#a w=480 h=90

=== auto margins centre
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="width:200px;height:10px;margin:0 auto"></div>
<div id=b style="width:200px;height:10px;margin-left:auto"></div>
<div id=c style="width:200px;height:10px;margin-right:auto;margin-left:30px"></div>
---

#b x=600
#c x=30

---

=== percentage height of a definite parent
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="height:200px"><div id=a style="height:50%"></div></div>
---
#a h=100

=== percentage height of an auto parent is auto
<body style="margin:0">
<div><div id=a style="height:50%"><div style="height:30px"></div></div></div>
---
#a h=30

=== quirks mode: percentage heights look through auto-height blocks

=== min-height and max-height
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="min-height:40px"></div>

<div id=c style="height:10px;min-height:30px;max-height:5px"></div>
---

#b h=20
#c h=30

=== width of a block with border-box and percentage
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="width:400px"><div id=a style="box-sizing:border-box;width:50%;padding:0 10px;height:5px"></div></div>
---

=== negative margins widen a block
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="width:400px;margin-left:100px"><div id=a style="margin:0 -20px;height:5px"></div></div>
---
#a x=80 w=440

=== over-constrained margins: margin-right is ignored
<body style="margin:0">

---
#a x=50 w=300
