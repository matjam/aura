=== scrolling a container moves its content, clamped to the extent
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=s style="overflow:auto;width:100px;height:100px;padding:10px;box-sizing:border-box">
<div id=a style="height:50px;background:#ff0000"></div>
<div id=b style="height:50px;background:#00ff00"></div>
<div id=c style="height:50px;background:#0000ff"></div>
</div>
---
pixel 50 15 ff0000
hit 50 15 #a
// extent: 10 + 150 + 10 bottom padding = 170, so at most 70 of scrolling
scrollat 50 50 0 1000 yes
pixel 50 85 0000ff
hit 50 85 #c
hit 50 95 #s
pixel 50 20 00ff00
hit 50 20 #b
scrollat 50 50 0 10 no
scrollat 50 50 0 -1000 yes
hit 50 15 #a

=== horizontal scroll extent includes the end padding

pixel 95 5 ff0000
