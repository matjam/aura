Positioning schemes (CSS 2.1 9.3, 10.3.7, 10.6.4; sticky).

=== relative offsets do not move siblings
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="position:relative;left:10px;top:20px;height:30px"></div>
<div id=b style="height:10px"></div>
---
#a x=10 y=20
#b y=30

=== relative right and bottom
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="position:relative;right:10px;bottom:5px;height:30px"></div>
---
#a x=-10 y=-5

=== relative percentage offsets
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="height:200px"><div id=a style="position:relative;left:10%;top:10%;height:10px"></div></div>
---
#a x=80 y=20

=== relative children move with the parent
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="position:relative;left:10px;top:10px"><div id=c style="height:10px;margin-left:5px"></div></div>
---
#c x=15 y=10

=== absolute in the initial containing block
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="position:absolute;left:10px;top:20px;width:100px;height:50px"></div>
<div id=b style="position:absolute;right:10px;bottom:20px;width:100px;height:50px"></div>
---
#a x=10 y=20
#b x=690 y=530

=== absolute against a positioned ancestor's padding box
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="position:relative;margin:50px;border:5px solid;padding:10px;height:200px">
<div id=a style="position:absolute;left:0;top:0;width:10px;height:10px"></div>
<div id=b style="position:absolute;right:0;bottom:0;width:10px;height:10px"></div>
</div>
---
#a x=55 y=55
#b x=735 y=265
