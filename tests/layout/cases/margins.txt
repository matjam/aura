Margin collapsing (CSS 2.1 8.3.1).

=== adjacent siblings: larger positive wins
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="height:50px;margin-bottom:20px"></div>
<div id=b style="height:10px;margin-top:30px"></div>
---
#a y=0 h=50
#b y=80

=== positive and negative add
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=a style="height:50px;margin-bottom:20px"></div>
<div id=b style="height:10px;margin-top:-5px"></div>
---
#b y=65

=== two negatives: most negative wins
<body style="margin:0">
<div style="height:100px"></div>
<div id=a style="height:50px;margin-bottom:-10px"></div>
<div id=b style="height:10px;margin-top:-20px"></div>
---
#a y=100
#b y=130

---

#c y=35

=== inline-block margins do not collapse
