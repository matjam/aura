Shadow trees: boxes come from the flat tree (CSS Scoping 3, DOM 4.2.2.4).
The shadow roots here are declarative (<template shadowrootmode>).

=== a host shows its shadow tree, not its children
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=h><template shadowrootmode=open><div id=s style="height:30px"></div></template><div id=light style="height:50px"></div></div>
<div id=after style="height:10px"></div>
---
#s y=0 h=30
#after y=30

=== a default slot takes the children in order
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=h><template shadowrootmode=open><div id=top style="height:10px"></div><slot></slot><div id=bottom style="height:10px"></div></template><div id=a style="height:20px"></div><div id=b style="height:5px"></div></div>
---
#top y=0
#a y=10 h=20
#b y=30 h=5
#bottom y=35

=== named slots place children where the slots are
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=h><template shadowrootmode=open><slot name=second></slot><slot name=first></slot></template><div id=one slot=first style="height:10px"></div><div id=two slot=second style="height:20px"></div></div>
---
#two y=0
#one y=20

=== slot fallback shows when nothing is assigned
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=h><template shadowrootmode=open><slot name=x><div id=fb style="height:15px"></div></slot><div id=after style="height:5px"></div></template><div id=a style="height:20px"></div></div>
---
#fb y=0 h=15
#after y=15

=== a slot is display: contents; its children lay out in the slot's parent
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=h><template shadowrootmode=open><div style="display:flex"><slot></slot></div></template><div id=a style="width:30px;height:10px"></div><div id=b style="width:40px;height:10px"></div></div>
---
#a x=0 y=0
#b x=30 y=0

=== styles inside the shadow tree do not reach the light children
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=h><template shadowrootmode=open><style>div { height: 40px; }</style><div id=s></div><slot></slot></template><div id=a style="width:10px"></div></div>
---
#s h=40
#a y=40 h=0

=== :host and ::slotted style the host and its slotted children
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=h><template shadowrootmode=open><style>:host { display: block; padding-top: 7px; } ::slotted(p) { margin: 0; height: 12px; }</style><slot></slot></template><p id=a></p></div>
---
#h h=19
#a y=7 h=12

=== nested shadow trees
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=outer><template shadowrootmode=open><div id=mid style="padding-left:5px"><template shadowrootmode=open><div id=deep style="height:9px"></div><slot></slot></template><slot></slot></div></template><div id=light style="height:3px"></div></div>
---
#deep x=5 y=0 h=9
#light x=5 y=9 h=3

=== hit testing reaches shadow content; a link around a slot holds slotted text
<body style="margin:0">
<div id=h><template shadowrootmode=open><div id=s style="height:20px"></div><a href="http://example.com/x" style="display:block"><slot></slot></a></template><div id=a style="height:20px"></div></div>
---
hit 5 5 #s
hit 5 25 #a
link 5 25 http://example.com/x

=== inline content flows through a slot
<body style="margin:0;width:200px">
<p id=p style="margin:0">ab<span id=h><template shadowrootmode=open>[<slot></slot>]</template><b id=b>cd</b></span>ef</p>
---
#p h=16 lines=1
#b x=48
